Our Philosophy

18th-Century Elegance.
Organic Texas Soul.

A bohemian curation of American and European antiques fills our dining room, blending old-world warmth with effortless Hill Country charm. Step outdoors beneath vine-draped pergolas where lavender, native flora, and our flourishing kitchen herb garden perfume the evening air.

Local and organic ingredients reshape our plates as the seasons peak and wane—from bright Gulf Coast crudo and house-extruded pastas to Texas Wagyu and Axis venison over live fire.
